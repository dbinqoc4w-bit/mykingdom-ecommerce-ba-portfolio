# Wireframe – Product Detail Page

## Overview

This wireframe represents the Product Detail Page (M-05) of the MyKingdom E-commerce Platform.

The design aims to provide customers with complete product information, support purchase decisions, and improve the online shopping experience.

## Main Components

- Product image gallery
- Product name and brand
- Product pricing and promotions
- Stock availability status
- Quantity selector
- Add to Cart button
- Wishlist feature
- Product specifications
- Related products section
- Customer reviews and ratings
